How it works

Turning this on downloads an 89 MB image model from Hugging Face once. After that, every picture and video thumbnail you scroll past gets a score for how AI-made it looks, and anything above your cutoff is hidden behind a Show bar.

It makes mistakes, so teach it: click Show on something it hid and answer AI, hide it or Not AI. Hiding something it missed (the Hide slop button, or right-click) counts as an AI rating. Each rating retrains it on this computer.

Save to a file exports your ratings as numbers that describe each picture, not the pictures themselves.

How it works

Labels are checked on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest; hashtags on those plus X, Reddit and LinkedIn.

The metadata check reads C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC "AI-generated" tags, and the settings Midjourney, DALL·E, ChatGPT, Firefly, Stable Diffusion and ComfyUI leave behind. Facebook and Instagram strip these on upload; restaurant sites, ad networks and image hosts often don't. Images are re-read from wherever the page got them; nothing is sent anywhere else.
