It's on. Its settings are in Safari, under the Hide the Slop button in the toolbar.

  1. Click Open Safari Settings below.
  2. In Extensions, tick Hide the Slop.
  3. Allow it on all websites, so it can hide slop wherever you go.

Hides

Posts with AI labels and AI hashtags, pictures carrying the hidden note AI tools leave in them, and pictures your robot thinks are AI-made. Everything it hides keeps a bar with a Show button, so you can always look.

Teach

Your robot is a small AI that runs on your Mac, off until you turn it on in the extension's settings. It makes mistakes, so teach it: click Show on something it hid and answer AI, hide it or Not AI, or hide one it missed with the Hide slop button. Each rating retrains it on your Mac.

Share

Sharing is off unless you turn it on. Then each picture you rate sends a fingerprint of it and your answer, never the picture, its web address or anything about you.
